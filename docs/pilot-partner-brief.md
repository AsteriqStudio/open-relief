# Open Relief private-pilot partner brief

## What exists today

Open Relief is a polished Android walkthrough and private sandbox for the proposed direct-to-creditor relief workflow. An invited tester can create a test account, upload a synthetic overdue bill, pass it through mock OCR and administrator review, and see a simulated direct-creditor payment result. The platform does not send money to recipients.

The current build does not charge cards, disburse funds, validate a real creditor account, or issue a tax receipt. It is suitable for product demonstrations and synthetic-data workflow testing only.

The [partner discovery shortlist](partner-discovery-shortlist.md) names payment, operator, and sponsor categories to qualify before a real pilot. It does not imply that any organization is affiliated with Open Relief.

## What we need from a first-pilot partner

We are looking for one narrow pilot: one market, one or two bill categories, a limited creditor set, and an accountable operator. A sponsor or institutional partner should be able to describe its intended campaign budget, matching policy, fee funding, commercial service arrangement, reporting needs, and dispute owner.

A creditor or bill-pay partner should be able to confirm merchant identity, account-reference format, payment confirmation, rejected-payment handling, duplicate-payment prevention, and reconciliation cadence.

## Questions for discovery

1. Which overdue bill categories create the most urgent preventable hardship?
2. Which creditors can validate account references and confirm settlement reliably?
3. Who funds processor fees when a donor does not choose fee coverage?
4. Who owns refunds, chargebacks, disputes, and recipient support?
5. What reporting, privacy, retention, and accessibility requirements apply?
6. What evidence would make the pilot commercially viable after processor, payout, support, fraud, and legal costs?

## Pilot boundary

No real funds or personal bills enter the private pilot. A public launch requires signed partner arrangements, legal review of the funds flow, secure operations, tested provider integrations, and explicit approval before live charges or disbursements are enabled. See [public launch readiness](public-launch-readiness.md) for the complete release gate.
